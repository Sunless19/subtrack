## What's new in 0.3.0

**Contracts**
- Add a contract end date and notice period; SubTrack reminds you before the **last day to cancel** so it doesn't renew

**Never miss a reminder**
- If the PC was off on a payment day, you get a *payment while you were away* notification at the next start (up to 7 days back)

**Pause and intro prices**
- **Skip next payment** with one click, or pause between two dates (Edit → More options)
- **Introductory price** until a date; totals use it while it lasts and you get a reminder before the regular price starts

**Where the money goes**
- **Spent so far** column: your share of every payment since the first one, at the price it was then
- Dashboard → *Over time*: the last 12 months and a 12-month forecast
- **Payment method** per subscription (card, PayPal, …) with a filter

**Quicker to add**
- Templates for ~60 common services: type "Netflix" or "Microsoft 365" and the category and billing cycle fill in
- Coloured letter badges next to every name

**Exchange rates**
- Settings → **Update rates online** loads today's European Central Bank rates (frankfurter.dev). Opt-in: SubTrack never goes online unless you click it.

**Upgrading**
- Run the new installer; your data from 0.1 / 0.2 is kept and upgraded automatically.

## Known limitation

A click on a real notification button has not been checked by hand yet (only automated tests). If a button does nothing, use the **Reminders** tab instead.
