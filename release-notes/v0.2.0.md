## What's new in 0.2.0

**Reminders**
- A reminder on the payment day itself, in addition to the one a few days before
- Windows notifications now have buttons: **Mark as paid**, **Remind me in 1 hour**, **Remind me tomorrow**
- The day-of notification stays on screen until you act on it
- New **Reminders** tab: the last 30 and next 30 days of payments, with their status (Paid, Reminded, Snoozed, Upcoming, Not marked as paid); mark payments paid there too

**Shared plans**
- Split a subscription between several people; every total uses your share

**Price history**
- Price changes are recorded; the list shows ▲/▼ % since the first price and Edit lists each change

**Calendar export**
- Export → *Calendar* writes the next 12 months of payments as an `.ics` file for Google Calendar or Outlook, with an alert the day before

**Finding things**
- Search box (Ctrl+F), category filter, *Show cancelled*, and sorting by any column; money columns sort correctly across currencies
- Cancelled subscriptions can be reactivated

**Other**
- A log file at `%APPDATA%\SubTrack\subtrack.log`
- Upgrading from 0.1.0 keeps all your data; just run the new installer

## Known limitation

The notification buttons were tested automatically, but a click on a real notification has not been checked by hand yet. If a button does nothing, open the **Reminders** tab and mark the payment paid there.

If Windows Focus / *Do not disturb* is on, notifications go straight to the Notification Center (Win+N) without popping up.
